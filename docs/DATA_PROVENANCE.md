# Data and dependency provenance

The demo database is described as a slim Synthea-derived warehouse, with its observation table sampled to reduce distribution size. The dbt models and loader are in `warehouse/` and `scripts/load_raw.py`. CI generates a separate dataset using Synthea 4.0.0, but the repository does not establish that the committed database was generated with that version or provide a complete manifest to reproduce it exactly. Its SHA-256 is `195dc959f6231178b5ffd60f0056ff4a39d29ff8f9f949b80dd91fd1fd50cda0`.

The three public example datasets below are derived from clinical observations; they are not synthetic. Their UCI records state that they are distributed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). See [third-party notices](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) for attribution and source links. UCI describes replacing names and Social Security numbers with dummy values in the Heart Disease data; that statement is not an independent assessment of re-identification risk for these files.

The key-free examples named `synthetic_*.csv` are constructed examples, separate from these clinical datasets. Hashes identify the checked files; they do not establish consent, scientific validity, or provenance for their source records.

The project MIT license covers project software; dependencies, data, and model weights have separate terms. The full application pins `scikit-survival==0.28.0`, whose package declares GPL-3.0-or-later. The effect on a particular distribution requires review of its contents and legal obligations. Neither standalone library directly declares scikit-survival. The direct and transitive dependencies, bundled binaries, datasets, and model weights still require a distribution-specific license review. The optional identifier model is fetched from a third party without an immutable revision pin; its model card currently declares Apache-2.0. Confirm the exact revision and terms before distributing it. See [third-party notices](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) and the [release checklist](RELEASE_CHECKLIST.md).
